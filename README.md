# Zhaoxiang Liu | Academic & Team Website

This repository hosts my personal academic homepage and our interdisciplinary research and development team website.

## Website Structure

```text
/
├── index.html       # Personal academic homepage
├── team.html        # Team page
├── README.md
└── fig/             # Team and member images
    ├── team.jpg
    ├── zhaoxiang-liu.jpg
    ├── member-2.jpg
    └── member-3.jpg
```

## Pages

### Personal Homepage

`index.html`

The personal homepage contains information about:

- Education
- Research experience
- Skills
- Certificates
- Publications

The existing personal homepage is maintained independently and is not modified by the team page.

### Team Page

`team.html`

The team page presents our interdisciplinary research and development activities, including:

- Team introduction
- Team members and academic backgrounds
- Research and development responsibilities
- AI-driven drug discovery
- Website development
- Intelligent agent development
- Software development
- Multimodal AI and biological modeling
- Computational and experimental research
- Representative research and development outputs
- Collaboration information

## Images

Team-related images are stored in the `fig/` directory.

Recommended filenames:

```text
fig/team.jpg
fig/zhaoxiang-liu.jpg
fig/member-2.jpg
fig/member-3.jpg
```

When adding a new team member, place the member's photo in `fig/` and update the corresponding member card in `team.html`.

## Deployment

The website can be hosted directly with GitHub Pages.

The default personal homepage is:

```text
index.html
```

The team page is:

```text
team.html
```

No additional framework or build process is required.
